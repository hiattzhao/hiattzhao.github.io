title: "Day 117: Magdeburg to Bad Belzig"
layout: post

---

Yesterday was a tough riding day.

I had left Karolin’s place and went to a bakery in Magdeburg for breakfast. As soon as I finished breakfast it started to rain. The clouds must have been going in the same direction I was since it rained the whole day.

Even though I wore my rain jacket, I was soon soaked. My wool socks only prevented my feet from getting wet for a short period of time.

The worst of all is the raindrops that kept on pressing random buttons on my smartphone when I was using Google Maps. Even when I turned off the screen the raindrops would turn it back on again. It was very irritating.

Google Maps also directed me to dirt roads or hiking paths that I didn’t dare to ride on. Maybe I would have if it didn’t rain, but the mud would have made my ride very difficult. I ended up going on the main road, Route 246, after deciding to deal with the cars rather than the difficult road conditions.

I didn’t see any restaurants on the road until around 3 PM. I had a very late lunch consisted of a döner.

The constant rain somehow made me drink less water. By the time I arrived at my Warmshowers host’s yard in Bad Belzig I still had a bottle of water left. I had ridden 58.2 miles for the day even though Google Maps indicated an estimated 47 miles when I checked in the morning.

My host Heidi wasn’t home, but she gave me directions to turn on the water so I can use the sink and the shower in the back of the barn. That was all I needed.

I didn’t even eat dinner and I ended up sleeping in the camper rather than pitching my tent, which was more comfortable.

This morning, I woke up around 7:30. It might rain again today even though it’s not raining now. I’m not sure what route I’ll take to Berlin. I don’t trust Google Maps and the EuroVelo route might also take me on dirt trails. It’s an adventure for sure.
