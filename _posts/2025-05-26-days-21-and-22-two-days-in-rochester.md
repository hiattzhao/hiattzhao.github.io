In Rochester, I took two days of rest. The first day, I walked around downtown to High Falls, then Memorial Art Gallery, Image City Photography Gallery, Artisan Works, George Eastman Museum. I also passed by the Edward Boynton House, which was designed by Frank Lloyd Wright, and The Old Toad, a British Pub that I used to go to when I was an undergraduate student at the University of Rochester. I walked so much that day I thought I had gotten a blister but I didn’t.

Yesterday after leaving Peter’s house, I rode my bike back to downtown to take photos of The Little Theatre and The Eastman Theatre. I had a cappuccino at Java’s Cafe and breakfast at Jim’s On Main before cycling to the main campus of University of Rochester.

I was a student here twice, once undergraduate at the College and another time as a finance graduate student at Simon Business School. I enjoyed my time at the university overall despite having depression in the second half of my undergraduate years. I now look at my time here fondly.

I visited all the dorm buildings where I used to live. Since it was a Sunday, almost every building was closed, including the main Rush Rhees Library. I was able to walk into Hutchison Hall, the chemistry building where I spent a lot of time there completing my chemistry degree. I left my contact card on the bulletin board hoping someone would notice.

After visiting the main campus, I had a bowl of ramen at Tai Chi Bubble Tea in College Town. This area has changed a lot since I was last here ten years old. There are new stores and restaurants mainly catered to the students and staff of the university.

Around 1 PM, I arrived at my Warmshowers host’s house across the river from the university. Lindsey and Luke were very gracious hosts and made homemade pizza for dinner. I also made some cucumber with garlic as a side dish that turned out pretty good.

I woke up at 6:30 this morning. I was planning to ride about 30 miles to Red Rock Ponds RV Resort just outside of Holley, but Lindsey said that there are free camping spots along the canal. She recommended a place outside of Medina. I will have to ride about 47 miles if I want to camp there.
