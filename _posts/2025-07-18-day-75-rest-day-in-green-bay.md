Yesterday was a good rest day for me. I did laundry, inflated the tires on my bike, finished reading Mike’s Camino book, took a hike in the farm, and had wonderful meals that were cooked by Paul.

Mike and Paul were great hosts, not only to me but also to their three farm workers. Paul cooked a delicious chicken enchilada lunch for all of us. Then at dinner time, he cooked veggie burgers when Mike’s brother Joey and his daughter Erin came to visit.

The highlight of the day was when Joey invited me to see a play at the Watermark Theatre that night. Of course, I decided to go.

Joey, Erin, and I went to the wharf in North Rustico Harbour first before going to see the show.

The play was called “Dear Jack, Dear Louise.” It’s about a couple writing letters to each other during WWII and is based on a true story. It only had two actors and they performed very well. I don’t remember the last time I saw a play and this one was really good.

I arrived back at Mike and Paul’s farm at 10:15 PM. Today, I need to leave here around 7 AM and get to the ferry terminal at 1 PM to catch the 1:30 PM ferry from Wood Islands, PEI, to Caribou, Nova Scotia. The ferry leaves about every two hours and I want to get to my Warmshowers host’s place not too late.
