# Day 5 (August 21)

In the morning, I went to my favorite Osaka coffee shop, Marufuku, for a cup of coffee and an egg sandwich. I didn't know what else to do, but I knew I didn't want to stay in the dark hostel all day so I took the train to Sumiyoshi Shrine. It's one of the oldest Shinto shrines in Japan and has an unusual looking bridge. I also walked to the nearby Sumiyoshi Park, where there were some herons just chilling.

I didn't want to do anything in the afternoon due to the heat. I also realized that there aren't many things I want to do inn Osaka and I should have only booked 4 or 5 nights at this hostel. Oh well.
