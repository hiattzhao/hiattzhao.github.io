*Zhaoism*

On November 23, 2020, I was lying in bed trying to sleep. That's when inspiration struck and I made the decision to publish my autobiography entitled*Zhaoism*. The biography covered the first 25 years of my life and it was already finished in 2014. I had put it on its own website for a few years, but decided to take it down. Now, I want to publish it on Amazon as an eBook and as a paperback for a wider distribution.

The process took a while because I had to make a cover and add some things to the front matter and back matter of the book. I also uploaded the material multiple times just to make sure I did it right. Now, both editions of the book are available on Amazon.

The book is written in an interview styled format. I found writing my biography in the traditional paragraph form was hard and dull. The interview style was more light-hearted and easier to edit.

If you had met me before 2009 (that's when the biography ends) you might be included in the book. No guarantees though.

I'm so excited about this publication! I feel like it's giving a second birth to something I enjoyed dearly. I hope you get a chance to read it and let me know what you think.
