Today is my last day in King of Prussia, PA. This afternoon, I will board a plane and fly to Miami. It'll be a start of a new adventure.

I feel both freeing and a bit uncertain. Not having a home base means I don't know where I'll come back to. At the same time, I don't have to worry about when to be back. I certainly feel lighter than before and don't have many burdens holding me back, but the future does scare me just a little.

Over the past several days, I sorted and edited my 2025 and 2026 photographs. They are now included in my photo portfolio on this website. That's the most important thing I accomplished recently.

From the previous post, I wrote that I will be posting some photographs of the area that I'm currently living. So here are some photos of Wayne and King of Prussia.
