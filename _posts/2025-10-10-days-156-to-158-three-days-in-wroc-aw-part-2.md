I’ve spent another three days in Wrocław, but I didn’t do much except going to the Hydropolis, which is a science museum that focuses on water. I also went by the Centennial Hall and the Wrocław Multimedia Fountain. There weren’t too many English signs in Hydropolis so I missed out on some of the information that was presented. However, I did watch a few films in the museum that had English subtitles.

Yesterday, I went to the mall near the train station and went to the library to read a book. I didn’t feel like walking far from the hostel and was feeling a bit lazy. I decided to buy a Flixbus ticket to Prague, Czechia, so that I won’t get too bored in Wrocław. I’ll be leaving in a couple of hours.

The weather in Wrocław has been cloudy with the occasional drizzle in the afternoon. I need to go somewhere sunny and I hope the weather in Prague would be better.
