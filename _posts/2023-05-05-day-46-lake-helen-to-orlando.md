I arrived in Orlando around 11 AM and decided to eat a banh mi sandwich with a Vietnamese coffee boba tea at the Blended Bistro and Boba. The boba tea was very good.

Around 12:30 PM, I arrived at my Warmshowers host Jon’s apartment building after riding close to 40 miles for the day. Jon was very enthusiastic and told me about his cycling trips with his wife from a few years ago. I was impressed by his photographs that he took while cycling on his recumbent trike.
